Privacy

The short version: what you log in hebomb stays on your phone, with a copy in your own iCloud. We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and there is nothing to sell.

What hebomb stores, and where

Your workouts, weights, bodyweight, goals and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for your phone, hebomb also keeps a copy of that same data in your own iCloud, in a private space that only the hebomb app on your Apple Account can read, so a new or replacement phone can bring it all back. That copy is held by Apple under your Apple Account, never by us. If you use the Apple Watch app, the watch shows what the phone sends it and keeps no history of its own.

hebomb has no accounts and no servers. The app does not send your information to us, so we hold no copy of it. Two things leave your phone only because you choose them: what you share with friends you add, and feedback you send. Both are below.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional, and you choose what to allow when you connect it.

  • On the phone, hebomb reads your weight and the workouts you have recorded, so you do not have to type them in. For each workout it also reads your heart rate, the energy you burned and, for an outdoor one, its route to draw the map, and it reads your date of birth to work out your heart rate zones. If your watch did not record a workout you did in hebomb, the phone saves that workout to Apple Health, with its type and its start and end times and nothing else.
  • On the watch, hebomb reads your heart rate, active energy and distance during a session, and saves the session to Apple Health as a workout. For an outdoor run, walk, hike or ride it also uses the watch's location, with your permission, to record the route, which is saved with the workout in Apple Health and nowhere else.

Anything read from Apple Health stays on your devices. It is never used for advertising and never sent to us. The only part that can leave your phone is the kind and length of a workout, shown to friends you have added if you share your workouts with them (see Friends, below). You can change or remove access at any time in the Health app, under Sharing.

Motion and fitness

When you start a walk, run or hike on the phone, hebomb can read the phone's step counter, with your permission, to show the distance, pace and floors climbed while it runs. It is read on the phone, shown on screen and not kept. Nothing from it leaves the phone. You can turn it off at any time in Settings, under Privacy & Security, Motion & Fitness.

Camera, for counting shots

During a basketball session you can choose to have hebomb count your makes and attempts with the phone's back camera. With your permission, it looks at each frame on the phone to see the ball pass the rim, and lets it go. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent, and no picture or video ever leaves the phone. Only the count is kept, with that session. You can turn the camera off for hebomb at any time in Settings.

Friends

Friends is optional. Nothing is shared until you send an invite and the other person accepts it, and sharing always works both ways.

  • What a friend sees: the name on your Apple Account (or the name you typed in hebomb), which days you worked out in the last 7, whether each was lifting or cardio, and, unless you turn it off for that friend, each workout's name, length and the lifts in it, and that you are working out right now. Never your weights, sets, bodyweight, strength score, heart rate, routes or anything else.
  • Where it is kept: each friendship is a private shared space in the inviter's own iCloud, run by Apple. Each of you writes only your own days. We have no server in between and cannot see it. Friends of a friend never see each other, and nobody sees anyone's friend list.
  • Invite links: an invite opens a page on hebomb.com. Your first name and the marks for your last 7 days travel inside the link itself, in the part after the # sign, which a browser never sends to any server, so the page shows them without them ever reaching us. Anyone you give the link to can see that much. Unused invites run out after 7 days.
  • Stopping: turn off Share my workouts in hebomb to stop sharing with everyone, or remove a friend, which deletes your days from the space you shared. Nobody is told.

Bringing in a file

If you bring in a workout history file from another app, hebomb reads it on your phone and keeps what it finds with the rest of your data. The file is not sent anywhere.

Feedback you send

Settings has a Send feedback page. Nothing is sent unless you write something and tap Send. What goes is your message, up to three screenshots and one screen recording if you choose to add them, the email address if you choose to type one so we can reply, the app version and your phone model. None of your workouts, weights or health data goes with it. Feedback is kept in a private Google Sheet, with any screenshots and recording in a private Google Drive folder, that only hebomb's developer can see, used only to answer you and improve the app, and deleted on request: write to us and say so. We delete feedback once it has been dealt with, and in any case within two years. Google stores it, in the United States, under its standard terms for Google Workspace.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking code in the app.
  • No advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because we never receive any.

Purchases

hebomb Pro is bought through Apple with your Apple Account. Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, your email or your card. Apple tells the app only whether Pro is active.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous totals, such as how many people downloaded the app or how often it crashed. Those reports do not identify you and do not include anything you logged.

Backups and deleting your data

With iCloud on, hebomb keeps its copy in your own iCloud after every change, plus one copy a week for the last nine weeks. When hebomb is installed on a phone with nothing saved, it offers to bring that copy back. Settings, Backup and restore, Restore from iCloud lists every copy. You can also save a backup file from Settings, which goes wherever you choose to put it, and only there. Your usual iPhone backup may also include the app's data, under your own Apple Account.

To delete everything, use Settings, Backup and restore, Erase everything, which also removes hebomb's copies from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what is on the phone; its iCloud copy stays until you erase it in the app or remove it in your iPhone's Settings, under your name, iCloud, Storage. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing further to remove.

The website

hebomb.com has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep short-lived technical logs of visits, such as IP addresses, for security. We do not use them.

Your rights, including in the EU and UK

Spatial Design LLC is responsible for the one kind of personal information we hold: feedback you send. We hold it because you chose to send it (your consent), to answer you and improve the app. You can ask us for a copy, to correct it or to delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to help@hebomb.com. We will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Everything else hebomb keeps is on your devices and in your own iCloud, which we cannot reach. You can see, export and delete all of it yourself in the app: Settings, Backup and restore.

Children

hebomb is not aimed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone apart from feedback they choose to send.
