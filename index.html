Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Everything works on the iPhone alone, including logging from the lock screen. If you have an Apple Watch you can also log from your wrist, and hebomb records your heart rate while you train.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everyone who downloads hebomb during the launch gets all of it, Pro included, free for as long as they use the app. Nothing to sign up for and no card: it switches on by itself, and it comes back if you delete the app and download it again.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no password. Open the app and start.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, with a copy in your own iCloud so a new phone gets it all back. We never receive it, and you can save a backup file of your own from Settings whenever you like.

I am new to lifting. Is it for me?

Yes. Setup asks how long you have been lifting, and beginners start light on lifts that are easy to learn. Your first workout finds your starting point, every lift has a guide showing how to do it, and from then on hebomb tells you which workout to do and what weight to use for each set. It is not medical advice, so check with a doctor before you start.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment. hebomb is for iPhone and Apple Watch.
