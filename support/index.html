Help

A few answers, and a way to reach a person.

My weight or workouts are not showing up

Hebomb reads them from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Hebomb, and check that Weight and Workouts are switched on. If your scale or watch has its own app, make sure that app is writing to Apple Health too.

I paid for Pro and it is not on

Open Settings in Hebomb, tap Hebomb Pro, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How the free month works

Hebomb Pro starts with a free month. You confirm it with your Apple Account, pay nothing for a month, and are charged the yearly price after that unless you cancel first. Hebomb reminds you three days before the first charge if notifications are on. Everything else in the app is free and stays free.

Cancelling a subscription or getting a refund

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Moving to a new phone

Your data lives on your phone. Before you switch, open Settings in Hebomb and save a backup file, then restore from it on the new phone. Pro comes back with Restore purchases.

Something else

Write to help@hebomb.com. A real person reads it.
